# Motor Policy Wording — MT-PW v2023.1

## Introduction

#### About this policy

This policy wording, your schedule, your certificate of motor insurance and any endorsements shown on your schedule form your contract with us. Your schedule shows your level of cover (Comprehensive, or Third Party Fire and Theft), the drivers insured, the permitted use of the vehicle, the deductibles, and which endorsement set is attached to the current period of insurance.

The endorsement set that applies to a loss is the set attached to the period of insurance in which the loss happened. Endorsements change this wording, and where they differ, the endorsement applies.

#### Making a claim

Report any accident, theft or damage to us as soon as possible, and in any case within 7 days. Do not admit fault or offer payment to anyone involved.

An adjuster will assess your claim against the wording and endorsements in force on the date of the loss. Information given by claims handlers or any automated service is not a decision on your claim.

#### Cancellation

You may cancel within 14 days of receiving your documents for a full refund if no claim has been made. After that you may cancel at any time and receive a proportionate refund less an administration charge of $35, provided no claim has been made in the current period.

We may cancel by giving 7 days' notice for a valid reason, including non-payment, a change in risk we cannot insure, or misrepresentation. Cover ends at the time stated in our notice.

## Definitions

The words and phrases below have the same meaning wherever they appear in this policy wording, the schedule and any endorsement.

<!-- page 2 -->

**Comprehensive cover.** The level of cover that includes Sections 1 to 6 of this wording.

**Third Party Fire and Theft (TPFT).** The level of cover that includes Sections 1 and 2 only. TPFT does not include loss of or damage to your own vehicle other than by fire or theft.

**Deductible.** The amount you must pay towards each claim, deducted from the assessed loss before any sub-limit or limit is applied. Different deductibles apply to different sections, as shown in the limits table.

**Market value.** The cost of replacing your vehicle with one of the same make, model, age, mileage and condition immediately before the loss.

**Insured driver.** Any person shown on your certificate of motor insurance as entitled to drive, who holds a valid licence for the vehicle and is not disqualified.

**Your vehicle.** The motor vehicle shown on your schedule, including its standard accessories and spare parts.

**Permitted use.** The purposes for which your vehicle may be used, as shown on your certificate of motor insurance.

**Period of insurance.** The period shown on your schedule. Each renewal starts a new period of insurance, with its own schedule and endorsement set.

**Endorsement set.** The group of endorsements attached to a period of insurance at its start or renewal. MEND-2023 attaches to periods starting on or after 1 April 2023 and before 1 April 2025; MEND-2025 to periods starting on or after 1 April 2025.

**Sub-limit.** A cap on what we pay for a particular type of loss within a section, separate from the deductible and the policy limit.

<!-- page 3 -->

**Policy limit.** The most we will pay under a section for any one claim, shown in the limits table.

**Total loss.** Where the cost of repair is more than 60% of the market value, or the vehicle is stolen and not recovered within 21 days.

**Windscreen.** The windscreen, windows and sunroof glass of your vehicle, and the bodywork scratching resulting solely from their breakage.

**Bodily injury.** Physical injury to a person, including death or illness resulting from that injury.

**Accessories.** Parts or products specifically designed to be fitted to your vehicle, including in-car entertainment and navigation equipment permanently fitted.

**Keys.** Keys, key cards, fobs and any other device used to unlock or start your vehicle.

**Official flood warning.** A flood warning issued by a national or local government weather or environment agency for the area in which the vehicle is located.

## Exclusions Schedule

These exclusions apply to every section of this policy unless an endorsement in the endorsement set attached to the period of insurance says otherwise.

<a id="mx-1"></a>
### MX-1 — Unlicensed and unauthorised drivers

We do not insure any loss, damage or liability while your vehicle is being driven by anyone not shown as an insured driver on your certificate, or by anyone who does not hold a valid licence or is disqualified from driving.

<!-- page 13 -->

<a id="mx-2"></a>
### MX-2 — Alcohol and drugs

We do not insure any loss of or damage to your vehicle while it is being driven by a person whose blood or breath alcohol level exceeds the legal limit, or who is unfit through drugs, or who fails to provide a specimen when required. Liability to third parties remains insured as the law requires, and we may recover our payment from you.

<a id="mx-3"></a>
### MX-3 — Racing and track use

We do not insure any loss, damage or liability while your vehicle is being used for racing, competitions, rallies, speed trials, or on a racetrack or derestricted toll road.

<a id="mx-4"></a>
### MX-4 — Mechanical and electrical breakdown

We do not insure mechanical, electrical, electronic or computer breakdowns, failures or breakages, or wear and tear, or depreciation.

<a id="mx-5"></a>
### MX-5 — Use not permitted

We do not insure any loss, damage or liability while your vehicle is being used for a purpose not permitted by your certificate, including hire or reward, delivery or courier work, or ride-sharing unless declared.

<a id="mx-6"></a>
### MX-6 — Keys left in the vehicle

We do not insure theft or attempted theft of your vehicle, or its contents, while the keys are left in, on or in the immediate vicinity of the vehicle, or while the vehicle is left unlocked or with any window or roof open.

<a id="mx-7"></a>
### MX-7 — Deliberate damage

We do not insure damage caused deliberately by you or any insured driver, or by anyone acting with your knowledge.

<a id="mx-8"></a>
### MX-8 — War, terrorism and nuclear

We do not insure loss, damage or liability caused by war, terrorism, riot outside the permitted territory, or nuclear and radioactive contamination, other than as required by law.

<a id="mx-9"></a>
### MX-9 — Confiscation

We do not insure loss or damage arising from confiscation, requisition or destruction of your vehicle by or under the order of any government or public authority.

<!-- page 14 -->

<a id="mx-10"></a>
### MX-10 — Wrong fuel

We do not insure damage caused by putting the wrong fuel into your vehicle, unless the misfuelling endorsement is shown on your schedule.

<a id="mx-11"></a>
### MX-11 — Loss of use and indirect loss

We do not insure loss of use of your vehicle, or any indirect loss such as loss of earnings or the cost of hiring another vehicle, other than as provided by Extension 7.1.

<a id="mx-12"></a>
### MX-12 — Territory

We do not insure any loss, damage or liability outside the territorial limits shown on your schedule.

<a id="mx-13"></a>
### MX-13 — Pollution

We do not insure liability for pollution or contamination unless caused by a sudden, identifiable and unexpected incident.

<a id="mx-14"></a>
### MX-14 — Carrying passengers for payment

We do not insure any liability while your vehicle is carrying passengers for hire or reward, other than shared fuel costs.

<a id="mx-15"></a>
### MX-15 — Modifications not declared

We do not insure any loss, damage or liability if your vehicle has been modified from the manufacturer's standard specification and you have not told us about the modification.

<a id="mx-16"></a>
### MX-16 — Drivers' conditions

We do not insure any loss, damage or liability if a driver has a medical condition that should have been declared to the licensing authority and was not.

<a id="mgc-12"></a>

## Claims Conditions

<a id="mcc-1"></a>
### MCC-1 — Notification

You must tell us as soon as reasonably possible about any event that may lead to a claim, and in any event within 30 days. Theft, attempted theft, and malicious acts must be reported to the police within 24 hours.

<a id="mcc-2"></a>
### MCC-2 — Preventing further damage

You must take all reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage, including arranging emergency repairs. We will pay the reasonable cost of emergency repairs as part of an insured claim.

<a id="mcc-3"></a>
### MCC-3 — Evidence

You must provide, at your own expense, any reasonable evidence we ask for, including receipts, valuations, photographs, estimates, and a written account of the incident.

<a id="mcc-4"></a>
### MCC-4 — Admission of liability

You must not admit liability or offer or agree to settle any claim against you without our written permission.

<a id="mcc-5"></a>
### MCC-5 — Our rights

We may take over and conduct in your name the defence or settlement of any claim, and pursue any claim for our own benefit against any other person.

<!-- page 17 -->

<a id="mcc-6"></a>
### MCC-6 — Inspection

We may appoint an adjuster or loss assessor to inspect the damage. You must give them reasonable access to the home and the damaged property.

<a id="mcc-7"></a>
### MCC-7 — Settlement of buildings claims

We will pay the cost of repair or rebuilding to a standard equivalent to new, less the deductible, provided the work is carried out without unreasonable delay. If the work is not carried out, we will pay the reduction in market value, up to the cost of repair.

<a id="mcc-8"></a>
### MCC-8 — Settlement of contents claims

We will replace or repair damaged contents as new, less the deductible, except clothing and household linen, for which we will deduct an amount for wear and tear.

<a id="mcc-9"></a>
### MCC-9 — Matching items

We will not pay for replacing undamaged items forming part of a pair, set, suite or carpet where the damage is restricted to a clearly identifiable area.

<a id="mcc-10"></a>
### MCC-10 — Applying deductibles and sub-limits

For each claim we first assess the loss, then deduct the applicable deductible, and then apply any applicable sub-limit and finally the policy limit. Where a section or endorsement sets its own deductible, that deductible applies in place of the standard deductible, not in addition to it.

<a id="mcc-11"></a>
### MCC-11 — Wording in force

Every claim is assessed against this wording and the endorsement set shown on the schedule for the period of insurance in which the loss date falls. A later or earlier endorsement set does not apply to that loss, even where its wording is clearer.

<a id="mcc-12"></a>
### MCC-12 — Referral to an adjuster

Coverage decisions are made only by an authorised adjuster. Any automated or assisted triage provides information only and does not accept or decline a claim.
