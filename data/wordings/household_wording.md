# Household Policy Wording — HH-PW v2023.1

## Introduction

#### About this policy

This policy wording, your schedule and any endorsements shown on your schedule form the contract of insurance between you and us. Please read them together. Your schedule tells you which sections you have chosen, the sums insured, the deductibles that apply and which endorsement set is attached to the current period of insurance.

We will insure you against the loss, damage or liability described in this wording which happens during the period of insurance, provided you have paid the premium and kept to the terms and conditions. Words shown in bold type have the meanings given in the Definitions section.

Endorsements change the cover in this wording. Where an endorsement and this wording say different things, the endorsement applies. The endorsement set that applies to a loss is the set attached to the period of insurance in which the loss happened, as shown on the schedule for that period.

#### How to make a claim

Tell us as soon as possible about any incident that may lead to a claim. You can report a claim by telephone or online at any time. We will give you a claim reference, which you should quote in all correspondence.

An adjuster will review your claim against the wording, schedule and endorsements in force on the date of the loss. Nothing said by our claims handlers or any automated service is a decision on your claim until it is confirmed in writing by an adjuster.

#### Cancellation rights

You may cancel this policy within 14 days of receiving your documents or the start of the period of insurance, whichever is later, and we will refund the premium in full provided no claim has been made.

<!-- page 2 -->

After the cooling-off period you may cancel at any time by contacting us. We will refund the premium for the unexpired part of the period of insurance, less an administration charge of $35, provided no claim has been made in the current period.

We may cancel this policy by giving you 14 days' notice in writing where there is a valid reason, such as non-payment of premium, a change in risk we cannot insure, or suspected fraud. If we cancel, cover ends at 00:01 on the date shown in our notice, and losses after that time are not insured.

#### Complaints

We aim to provide a high standard of service. If you are not satisfied, please contact our customer relations team, quoting your policy number. We will acknowledge your complaint within five working days and aim to resolve it within eight weeks.

If you remain dissatisfied, you may be able to refer your complaint to the insurance ombudsman or the regulator in your state. Making a complaint does not affect your legal rights.

## Definitions

The words and phrases below have the same meaning wherever they appear in this policy wording, the schedule and any endorsement.

**Accidental damage.** Sudden, unexpected and visible damage which has not been caused on purpose and which happens at a single identifiable time.

**Bodily injury.** Physical injury to a person, including death or illness resulting from that injury.

**Buildings.** The home, its fixtures and fittings, permanently installed swimming pools, tennis courts, drives, patios and terraces, walls, gates and fences, and domestic outbuildings, all within the premises.

<!-- page 3 -->

**Contents.** Household goods and personal property belonging to you or for which you are legally responsible, kept in the home, but not motor vehicles, caravans, trailers, watercraft, livestock, or any part of the buildings.

**Deductible.** The amount you must pay towards each claim. The deductible is deducted from the assessed loss before any sub-limit or sum insured is applied. A deductible is not a sub-limit and does not reduce the sub-limit.

**Endorsement.** A document that changes the cover provided by this wording. Endorsements are issued in sets; the set attached to a period of insurance is shown on the schedule for that period.

**Endorsement set.** The group of endorsements, identified by a set reference and version, attached to a period of insurance at its start or renewal. The 2023 set (HEND-2023) attaches to periods starting on or after 1 April 2023 and before 1 April 2025. The 2025 set (HEND-2025) attaches to periods starting on or after 1 April 2025.

**Escape of water.** Water suddenly escaping from a fixed water installation, domestic appliance, water tank, waterbed or aquarium within the home.

**Excess.** See Deductible.

**Fixed water installation.** Pipes, tanks, radiators, boilers and similar equipment permanently connected to the water supply or heating system of the home.

**Heave.** Upward movement of the ground beneath the buildings as a result of the soil expanding.

**Home.** The private dwelling at the address shown on the schedule, together with its garages and outbuildings, used for domestic purposes only.

**Landslip.** Downward movement of sloping ground.

<!-- page 4 -->

**Money.** Current coins and bank notes, postal and money orders, gift tokens and pre-paid cards, held for social or domestic purposes.

**Period of insurance.** The period shown on the schedule for which we have accepted your premium. Each renewal begins a new period of insurance with its own schedule and endorsement set.

**Personal possessions.** Clothing, jewellery, watches, and other items normally worn or carried by you, which belong to you or for which you are legally responsible.

**Policy limit.** The most we will pay for any one claim under a section, as shown on the schedule or in the limits table that forms part of this policy. A policy limit is distinct from both the deductible and any sub-limit.

**Premises.** The home and the land belonging to it, shown on the title deeds, at the address shown on the schedule.

**Schedule.** The document that gives details of you, the home, the sections in force, the sums insured, the deductibles, the endorsement set and the period of insurance.

**Settlement.** Movement of the buildings caused by the compaction of the ground beneath them under their own weight, normally within ten years of construction.

**Storm.** A period of violent weather with wind speeds of at least 55 miles per hour, or torrential rainfall of at least 25 millimetres per hour, or snow to a depth of at least 30 centimetres, or hail of such intensity that it causes damage to hard surfaces.

**Sub-limit.** A limit within a section that caps what we pay for a particular type of loss, regardless of the policy limit for that section. Sub-limits are shown in the endorsement set's schedule of sub-limits. The deductible is applied first and does not erode the sub-limit.

<!-- page 5 -->

**Subsidence.** Downward movement of the ground beneath the buildings, other than by settlement.

**Sum insured.** The amount shown on the schedule as the most we will pay under the relevant section.

**Terrorism.** Any act of any person or organisation involving the causing or threatening of harm, or putting the public in fear, for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes.

**Unoccupied.** Not lived in by you or a member of your family for more than 60 consecutive days.

**Valuables.** Jewellery, watches, items of gold, silver and other precious metals, furs, pictures, works of art and collections of stamps, coins or medals.

**We, us, our.** The insurer named on the schedule.

**You, your.** The person or persons named on the schedule as policyholder and members of their family who normally live in the home.

**Wear and tear.** Gradual deterioration through age or use, including rust, corrosion, rot and the gradual effects of light, weather and pollution.

## Exclusions Schedule

The exclusions below apply to every section of this policy unless an endorsement in the endorsement set attached to the period of insurance says otherwise. An exclusion's own text, including any exception written into it, is the policy's statement of what it does and does not exclude.

<a id="hx-1"></a>
### HX-1 — Wear and tear and gradual deterioration

We do not cover loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or anything that happens gradually, including continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of time, rot, rust, corrosion, mildew, or the action of light or atmosphere. This exclusion applies whether or not the deterioration was visible before the loss.

<!-- page 24 -->

<a id="hx-2"></a>
### HX-2 — Defective design, materials and workmanship

We do not cover the cost of putting right faulty or defective design, specification, materials or workmanship. We will cover resulting damage that is otherwise insured.

<a id="hx-3"></a>
### HX-3 — Flood, inundation and surface water

Flood, inundation and surface water are governed solely by the flood endorsement in the endorsement set attached to the period of insurance in which the loss happened. Where that endorsement does not give cover, the loss is not insured under any section of this wording. The 2023 set's flood endorsement is HFL-23; the 2025 set's is HFL-25. The two are not interchangeable: the endorsement to apply is the one on the schedule for the period in which the loss date falls.

<a id="hx-4"></a>
### HX-4 — Settlement and new structures

We do not cover loss or damage caused by settlement, shrinkage, or new structures bedding down, or by the normal movement of a building within ten years of construction.

<a id="hx-5"></a>
### HX-5 — Mould and fungus

We do not cover loss or damage caused by mould, fungus or wet rot, except where it results directly from an escape of water that is itself insured, in which case cover is limited to the mould sub-limit in the endorsement set's schedule of sub-limits.

<a id="hx-6"></a>
### HX-6 — Pollution and contamination

We do not cover loss or damage caused by pollution or contamination, unless caused by a sudden, identifiable, unintended and unexpected incident during the period of insurance.

<a id="hx-7"></a>
### HX-7 — Vermin and insects

We do not cover loss or damage caused by insects, vermin, rodents, birds, or by any animal owned by or in the care of you or your family, other than as provided by Accidental Damage Extension HAD where it is attached.

<a id="hx-8"></a>
### HX-8 — Frost in unheated premises

We do not cover frost damage to fixed water installations while the home is unoccupied unless the heating system was maintained at a minimum of 12 degrees Celsius day and night, or the water supply was turned off and the system drained.

<!-- page 25 -->

<a id="hx-9"></a>
### HX-9 — Storm damage to gates, fences and hedges

We do not cover loss or damage to gates, hedges and fences caused by storm, wind, rain, hail, snow or frost, or by flood in any form.

<a id="hx-10"></a>
### HX-10 — Deliberate acts

We do not cover loss or damage deliberately caused by you, your family, or anyone acting on your behalf or with your knowledge.

<a id="hx-11"></a>
### HX-11 — War and terrorism

We do not cover loss, damage, cost or liability caused by war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or by terrorism.

<a id="hx-12"></a>
### HX-12 — Nuclear and radioactive contamination

We do not cover loss, damage, cost or liability caused by ionising radiation or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, or the radioactive, toxic or explosive properties of any nuclear assembly.

<a id="hx-13"></a>
### HX-13 — Sonic bangs

We do not cover loss or damage caused by pressure waves from aircraft or other flying devices travelling at or above the speed of sound.

<a id="hx-14"></a>
### HX-14 — Business use

We do not cover loss, damage or liability arising from any trade, profession or business carried on at the home, other than clerical work, unless Home Business Endorsement HBU is attached and shown on the schedule.

<a id="hx-15"></a>
### HX-15 — Theft from let property

We do not cover theft or attempted theft from any part of the home let or sub-let to tenants, lodgers or paying guests, unless there was forcible and violent entry or exit.

<a id="hx-16"></a>
### HX-16 — Electronic data and cyber events

We do not cover loss or damage to any computer, electronic equipment or data caused by any computer virus, hacking, or the failure of any equipment to correctly recognise a date.

<a id="hx-17"></a>
### HX-17 — Groundwater and water table

We do not cover loss or damage caused by water rising from the water table or groundwater percolating through floors or basement walls, whether or not following rainfall. This exclusion applies in addition to, and is not overridden by, any flood endorsement.

<!-- page 26 -->

<a id="hx-18"></a>
### HX-18 — Pets

We do not cover loss or damage caused by chewing, scratching, tearing or fouling by pets or domestic animals.

<a id="hx-19"></a>
### HX-19 — Unoccupancy

We do not cover loss or damage caused by escape of water, escape of oil, theft, attempted theft, or malicious acts while the home is unoccupied, unless you have told us and we have agreed in writing.

<a id="hx-20"></a>
### HX-20 — Indirect loss

We do not cover any loss or expense that is not directly caused by the event that led to your claim, such as loss of earnings, time taken to prepare a claim, or reduction in market value after repair.

<a id="gc-11"></a>

## Claims Conditions

<a id="cc-1"></a>
### CC-1 — Notification

You must tell us as soon as reasonably possible about any event that may lead to a claim, and in any event within 30 days. Theft, attempted theft, and malicious acts must be reported to the police within 24 hours.

<a id="cc-2"></a>
### CC-2 — Preventing further damage

You must take all reasonable steps to prevent further loss or damage, including arranging emergency repairs. We will pay the reasonable cost of emergency repairs as part of an insured claim.

<a id="cc-3"></a>
### CC-3 — Evidence

You must provide, at your own expense, any reasonable evidence we ask for, including receipts, valuations, photographs, estimates, and a written account of the incident.

<a id="cc-4"></a>
### CC-4 — Admission of liability

You must not admit liability or offer or agree to settle any claim against you without our written permission.

<a id="cc-5"></a>
### CC-5 — Our rights

We may take over and conduct in your name the defence or settlement of any claim, and pursue any claim for our own benefit against any other person.

<a id="cc-6"></a>
### CC-6 — Inspection

We may appoint an adjuster or loss assessor to inspect the damage. You must give them reasonable access to the home and the damaged property.

<a id="cc-7"></a>
### CC-7 — Settlement of buildings claims

We will pay the cost of repair or rebuilding to a standard equivalent to new, less the deductible, provided the work is carried out without unreasonable delay. If the work is not carried out, we will pay the reduction in market value, up to the cost of repair.

<!-- page 29 -->

<a id="cc-8"></a>
### CC-8 — Settlement of contents claims

We will replace or repair damaged contents as new, less the deductible, except clothing and household linen, for which we will deduct an amount for wear and tear.

<a id="cc-9"></a>
### CC-9 — Matching items

We will not pay for replacing undamaged items forming part of a pair, set, suite or carpet where the damage is restricted to a clearly identifiable area.

<a id="cc-10"></a>
### CC-10 — Applying deductibles and sub-limits

For each claim we first assess the loss, then deduct the applicable deductible, and then apply any applicable sub-limit and finally the policy limit. Where a section or endorsement sets its own deductible, that deductible applies in place of the standard deductible, not in addition to it.

<a id="cc-11"></a>
### CC-11 — Wording in force

Every claim is assessed against this wording and the endorsement set shown on the schedule for the period of insurance in which the loss date falls. A later or earlier endorsement set does not apply to that loss, even where its wording is clearer.

<a id="cc-12"></a>
### CC-12 — Referral to an adjuster

Coverage decisions are made only by an authorised adjuster. Any automated or assisted triage provides information only and does not accept or decline a claim.
