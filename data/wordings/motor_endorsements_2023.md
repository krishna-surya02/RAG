# Motor Endorsement Set 2023 — MEND-2023 v2023.1

<!-- page 1 -->

This endorsement set attaches to motor periods of insurance starting on or after 1 April 2023 and before 1 April 2025. It forms part of the policy only for a period of insurance whose schedule shows MEND-2023. Each endorsement below is version v2023.1.

## MFL-23 v2023.1 — Flood and Storm Water Damage to the Vehicle

<a id="mfl-23-1"></a>
### MFL-23 §1 — Scope

This endorsement governs loss of or damage to the Vehicle caused by flood, storm water, or the ingress of water from any external source.

<a id="mfl-23-2-1"></a>
### MFL-23 §2.1 — Indemnity for flood damage

Loss of or damage to the Vehicle caused by flood or storm water is indemnified under Section 3 (Accidental damage) for Comprehensive cover only, subject to the own-damage deductible stated in the limits table.

<a id="mfl-23-2-2"></a>
### MFL-23 §2.2 — Deliberate entry into water

No indemnity is provided where the Vehicle is deliberately driven into water of a depth exceeding the manufacturer's stated wading depth, or into water which a reasonable driver would have recognised as unsafe.

## MWS-23 v2023.1 — Windscreen Endorsement

<a id="mws-23-1"></a>
### MWS-23 §1 — Windscreen deductible

The windscreen deductible is $100 per claim, and the windscreen sub-limit is $1,500 per claim, as stated in the limits table.

## MPA-23 v2023.1 — Personal Accident Endorsement

<a id="mpa-23-1"></a>
### MPA-23 §1 — Benefit

The personal accident sub-limit under Section 5 is $25,000 per person. The medical expenses sub-limit under Section 6 is $2,000 per person.
