# Household Endorsement Set 2025 — HEND-2025 v2025.1

<!-- page 1 -->

This endorsement set applies to household periods of insurance starting on or after 1 April 2025. It is part of your policy only for a period of insurance whose schedule shows HEND-2025. Each endorsement below is version v2025.1.

## HFL-25 v2025.1 — Flood, Surface Water and Storm Surge Endorsement

<a id="hfl-25-1"></a>
### HFL-25 §1 — What we mean by flood

In this endorsement, flood means water from any source outside your home that covers normally dry ground. Flood includes rivers and streams bursting their banks, the sea and storm surge, and surface water from heavy rain or rainfall that collects on the ground or runs off roads, gardens and fields into your home. Flood damage from heavy rain is flood, even if no river or sea is involved.

<a id="hfl-25-2-1"></a>
### HFL-25 §2.1 — Flood damage is not insured

We do not insure loss or damage caused by flood, including surface water from heavy rainfall, rising rivers and storm surge, unless Flood Plus is shown on your schedule. This applies to your buildings and your contents, and whether or not a storm happened at the same time.

<a id="hfl-25-2-2"></a>
### HFL-25 §2.2 — Flood Plus (optional)

If your schedule shows Flood Plus, we insure flood damage to your buildings and contents up to the Flood Plus sub-limit in the 2025 Schedule of Sub-limits (HSL-25), after the flood deductible in §2.3. Flood Plus is not available where your home is in a designated flood zone.

<a id="hfl-25-2-3"></a>
### HFL-25 §2.3 — Flood deductible

Each flood claim under Flood Plus has a flood deductible of $5,000. It replaces your standard buildings or contents deductible, and it is taken off the assessed loss before the Flood Plus sub-limit is applied.

<a id="hfl-25-3"></a>
### HFL-25 §3 — What you must do

Tell us about flood damage within 30 days, move valuables upstairs if a flood warning is issued, and keep damaged items for our adjuster to inspect where you can.

## HAD-25 v2025.1 — Accidental Damage Extension

<a id="had-25-1"></a>
### HAD-25 §1 — Cover

If your schedule shows accidental damage, your buildings and contents are also insured for accidental damage, after the accidental damage deductible in the limits table.

<a id="had-25-2"></a>
### HAD-25 §2 — What is not insured

Damage by pets, wear and tear, cleaning or repair, or electrical or mechanical breakdown.

## HBU-25 v2025.1 — Home Business Endorsement

<a id="hbu-25-1"></a>
### HBU-25 §1 — Cover

If your schedule shows home business cover, Exclusion HX-14 does not apply to office work at home, and business equipment is insured as contents up to $15,000.

## HSL-25 v2025.1 — Schedule of Sub-limits 2025

<a id="hsl-25-1"></a>
### HSL-25 §1 — Sub-limits

These sub-limits apply to periods of insurance carrying HEND-2025: Flood Plus sub-limit (HFL-25 §2.2): $25,000 per event. Mould sub-limit (HX-5): $7,500 per period of insurance. Valuables (Extension 2.3): $12,500 in total. Trace and access (Extension 1.14): $5,000. Each sub-limit applies after the deductible and is separate from the policy limit.
