# Motor Endorsement Set 2025 — MEND-2025 v2025.1

<!-- page 1 -->

This endorsement set applies to motor periods of insurance starting on or after 1 April 2025. It is part of your policy only for a period of insurance whose schedule shows MEND-2025. Each endorsement below is version v2025.1.

## MFL-25 v2025.1 — Flood Damage to Your Car

<a id="mfl-25-1"></a>
### MFL-25 §1 — What this covers

This endorsement sets out when flood damage to your car is insured, including water from rivers, the sea, heavy rain and flooded roads.

<a id="mfl-25-2-1"></a>
### MFL-25 §2.1 — Flood damage insured

We insure flood damage to your car under Section 3 if you have Comprehensive cover, after the flood deductible in §2.4.

<a id="mfl-25-2-2"></a>
### MFL-25 §2.2 — Car left in a flood warning area

We do not insure flood damage if your car was left parked in an area covered by an official flood warning that was issued more than 12 hours before the flood reached your car, and it was reasonably possible to move it.

<a id="mfl-25-2-3"></a>
### MFL-25 §2.3 — Driving into flood water

We do not insure damage caused by driving your car into flood water, including flooded roads and fords.

<a id="mfl-25-2-4"></a>
### MFL-25 §2.4 — Flood deductible

The flood deductible is $1,000 per claim. It replaces your own-damage deductible for flood claims.

## MHA-25 v2025.1 — Hail Endorsement

<a id="mha-25-1"></a>
### MHA-25 §1 — Hail deductible

Hail damage to your car has a hail deductible of $500, which replaces your own-damage deductible for hail claims. Paintless dent repair will be used where suitable.

## MWS-25 v2025.1 — Windscreen Endorsement

<a id="mws-25-1"></a>
### MWS-25 §1 — Windscreen deductible

The windscreen deductible is $150 per claim and the windscreen sub-limit is $1,500 per claim, as shown in the limits table.

## MPA-25 v2025.1 — Personal Accident Endorsement

<a id="mpa-25-1"></a>
### MPA-25 §1 — Benefit

The personal accident sub-limit under Section 5 is $25,000 per person. The medical expenses sub-limit under Section 6 is $2,500 per person.
